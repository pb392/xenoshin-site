# Which bosses each archetype plays

Around the Columbus Open (November 7 to 8, 2026), these are the bosses each archetype plays. A slash means the deck plays any of those bosses.

## Hades decks

Hades Sloth plays Neandersloth CoKhione. Hades Trifernal plays Trifernal CoHades or Sulfury.

## Apollo and Athena decks

Apollo Celestleo plays Celestleo. Athena Dimenshen plays Dimenshen CoAthena.

## Fish and Fire decks

Fish plays Apheros CoAphrodite, Magneros CoAphrodite or Lochagon. Sulfury Fire Discard plays Sulfury.

## Dionysus decks

Dio Duggy (Dionysus Duggy) plays Neandersloth CoKhione, Duggabyss or Centaurbor CoDionysus.
