# Meta overview after the Columbus Open

## Most played decks

The current meta is very diverse, with many strong decks being played, but at the last event, the Columbus Open, the majority of the decks played were Athena Dimenshen, Apollo Celestleo, Hades Trifernal, Fish, Hades Sloth, and Sulfury in that order.

## Top finishers

The main topping decks were Hades Sloth, Fish, Dionysus Duggy, and a few scattered other decks, with the winner playing Fish.

## Bosses define the meta

Overall, the meta is less defined by the decks that are played, but rather by the bosses that each deck played. Celestleo, Dimenshen, Neandersloth, and Fish (as a whole) are all very difficult bosses to beat, and if any deck is able to have one of them stay on the board, the tempo of the game sways completely in their favor.
