# 2026 Elestrals World Championship

## Champion

The first Elestrals World Championship took place in 2026, and Elestrals published the results on March 27, 2026. Mike Thomas, who plays under the name Trishula, won and became the first Elestrals World Champion. He won with a Celestleo deck.

## Top archetypes

Celestleo's Pride was the leading archetype at the event. It builds around the Solar Elestral Celestleo and uses the Foretell mechanic to find the cards it needs. Fish Nexus was the other heavily represented archetype. It is a Water deck in which Apheros and Magneros work together to fill the board.

## Elements and featured cards

Because Celestleo's Pride and Fish Nexus led the field, Solar and Water were the elements most featured among the top Elestrals at Worlds. Even so, the single most represented Elestral was Cyanectar, Champion of Dionysus, because it can search out key Runes such as Poison Tipped Arrow.

## Season timing

The event closed the 2026 season. The results article says the 2027 season starts with online Premier events.
